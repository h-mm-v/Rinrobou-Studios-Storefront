General Info

  • Please provide at least 1-2 references of the character, pose, outfit, and other details you'd like to be included. Written descriptions are appreciated. The more detail you provide us, the better!
  • We will NOT design a character or outfit from scratch unless specified in the service details.
  • We have the right to refuse a commission if we are uncomfortable or not confident enough to accommodate the request.
  • We do not offer lineart only.
  • Payment is required before the initial sketch is completed. We will NOT continue work on the commission until the payment has been sent.
  • Once work has begun and a WIP has been sent, we will not give a refund. However, in the case that we are unable to complete your commission, a refund can be arranged.
  • We will NOT accept any big revisions after the refined line art stage and especially once the final files have been sent.

Communication

Our art process has 4 stages: sketch, line, base color, and shade/render. For skebs, we will only contact you after shade/rendering is finished.

  • Communication will be done in VGen DMs unless specified elsewhere in the request form.
  • Please be responsive to any updates and messages we send, especially if you have a deadline.
  • Feel free to ask for updates and we'll do our best to get back to you ASAP.

Timeline

Keep in mind that RinRobou is a team of two people. Thief handles account management and base color while RinJ is in charge of sketch, line, and shade/render. Work completion and updates may be affected depending on what is going on irl for the two of us.

  • It will take at least 1-2 weeks minimum, depending on how many other commissions we have. Check out the Work Queue on our profile to access our Trello board where we post commission updates.
  • Rush fees are determined based on how soon you would like our work upon sending the proposal. Our rush fees are between 30-50% of the original price depending on how urgent the deadline is. The sooner your deadline, the higher your rush fee will be.

Usage

  • You are not allowed to alter and claim the commission artwork as your own, use it for a NFT and the like, or for AI training. Upon discovery of any of these prohibited acts, legal action will be taken and you will be added to our blacklist.
  • Commissions are primarily for personal use and are okay to post/use as icons, banners, etc. on social platforms as long as you credit us, "@rinroboustudios".
  • Please mention commercial usage (monetized content, merchandise) in your request prior to proceeding with the commission so we may discuss the commercial fee.
  • We have the right to post and include your commission on our portfolio.
